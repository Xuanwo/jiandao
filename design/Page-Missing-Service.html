Reading and Experience

A few months ago I finished a new book, and in reviews I keep noticing words like "gripping" and "explosive." I didn't set out to write a gripping book, but that's what happened.

Reading and experience train your model of the world. And even if you forget the experience or what you read, its effect on your model of the world persists.

Your mind is like a compiled program you've lost the source of. It works, but you don't know why.

So what you want to do is to read things that you'll be glad to have compiled into your model of the world, even if you don't remember them.
